SkyCheck

Climate Monitoring Web App

SkyCheck is a web-based climate platform developed for the 2025 NASA Space Apps Challenge. It uses the Meteomatics API and NASA datasets to show temperature, humidity, wind, and sky conditions through a responsive interface.

With intuitive visualizations, SkyCheck helps users understand local climate patterns and prepare for extreme conditions. It supports agricultural planning, logistics, and travel decisions, aiding adaptation to environmental change.

Problem Statement

Unexpected weather can disrupt outdoor activities, but historical climate data is often hard to access and interpret. SkyCheck was created for the 2025 NASA Space Apps Challenge to provide clear, actionable weather insights for farmers, companies, and travelers, helping them plan and prepare for changing conditions.


Design Process

We designed both desktop and mobile interfaces in Figma with reusable components. The desktop version was implemented using React, HTML, and CSS, focusing on clear visualization of temperature, humidity, wind, and sky conditions. The mobile version informed how the interface could adapt to smaller screens.

The project featured a clean and interactive interface with components for temperature, humidity, wind speed, and sky conditions. It included a location selector, a dashboard for viewing real-time and historical data.

SkyCheck makes complex climate information accessible, helping users understand local weather patterns and prepare for extreme conditions. It supports agricultural planning, logistics, and travel decisions, offering practical insights and actionable information for real-world scenarios.
